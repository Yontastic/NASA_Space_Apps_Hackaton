Scientific Methodology & Official Open Data

Mathematical model, discard funnel, and verified space-agency / biodiversity portals

1. Multidimensional Gaussian similarity

Each physical dimension i (slope, roughness, thermal swing, elevation, traction, humidity) is scored with a Gaussian kernel around the target value:

s_i = exp( -0.5 * ((x_i - x_target) / tau_i)^2 )

The tolerance tau_i is tied to your active filter thresholds, and the weighted sum S = Σ w_i s_i / Σ w_i uses the weights you set. Environmental restrictions (NDVI, water, lithology, protected status, data coverage) then apply multiplicative penalties. Moving any control recalculates every percentage.

2. Four-stage discard funnel

  • Stage 1 - Biology & water: NDVI above limit or active surface water.
  • Stage 2 - Geology: lithology filter.
  • Stage 3 - Topography & mobility: slope, roughness, elevation and rover traction index.
  • Stage 4 - Climate: humidity and diurnal thermal swing.

3. Marker colours & hazards

Green = passes all filters, similarity ≥ 75% and open terrain. Yellow = caution (filter failure, score < 75% or managed reserve). Red = protected area or critical hazard. Hazard polygons are indicative footprints generated from site attributes; they are not official WDPA boundaries.

4. Rover simulation

A 600 m traverse is generated from the site's slope, roughness, humidity and thermal swing. Wheel friction mu = 0.15 + 0.6 × traction index; the run fails on rollover (> 32° tilt), loss of traction or battery depletion.

Welcome to TerraAnalog

What is TerraAnalog?

TerraAnalog matches craters, lava tubes, and paleolakes on the Moon and Mars with their closest geological twin sites on Earth using NASA, ESA, and USGS satellite data. Before any rover touches the ground, it checks whether testing there would harm fragile terrestrial ecosystems or violate UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

2. Protect Fragile Earth Ecosystems (UN SDGs)

Many of Earth's best Mars/Moon analogs—like Cuatro Ciénegas (living stromatolites) or the Atacama Yungay Core (ancient microbial crusts)—are fragile, irreplaceable habitats.

Whenever you select a site, a prominent Environmental & UN SDG Impact Alert appears on screen showing affected UN SDGs (SDG 6, 11, 13, 14, 15), WDPA status, vulnerable endemic species, and plain-language rover impact warnings.

SDG 15 Life on Land SDG 6 Clean Water SDG 13 Climate Action SDG 14 Life Below Water

3. Test the Rover Across Real Biomes (Dossier 3D)

Click any analog site and press Dossier 3D to drive a simulated rover across a 600 m traverse generated from that site's real slope, roughness, thermal swing, and wheel traction.

The simulator dynamically renders each site's true Biome—from white gypsum wetlands with turquoise thermal pools to dark volcanic basalt cones, rust-red regolith deserts, and polar ice sheets.
